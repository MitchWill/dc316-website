What Your Smart TV Says About You

The talk explores how Smart TVs from LG, Roku, and Samsung can expose significant privacy and security risks through the data they collect and transmit. It walks through a three-step network traffic analysis process that uncovered excessive data leakage, exposed tokens, and an exploitable CVE enabling unauthenticated TV control, while offering practical ways users can assess and better protect their own devices and digital privacy.

Presented by Matt Palmer

What we do

DEFCON Group 316 (DC316) is Wichita's local DEF CON group — a community of hackers, security professionals, students, and the simply curious, meeting monthly in the 316. We run hands-on talks, live demos, and CTF-style events for all skill levels, from total beginners to seasoned pros. Come learn something, share something, or just hang out with people who get it.

Our October class walked through a real malware sample from the HackTheBox Holmes CTF, from basic file analysis through full static analysis on Linux.

Who we are

DEFCON Group 316 (DC316) is Wichita's local DEFCON group — a community of hackers, security professionals, students, and the simply curious, meeting monthly in the 316. We run hands-on talks, live demos, and CTF-style events for all skill levels, from total beginners to seasoned pros. Come learn something, share something, or just hang out.
